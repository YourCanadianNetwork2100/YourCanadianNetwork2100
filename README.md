### Who I Am

8 years of hands on experience building, testing and breaking systems across web platforms, real time game environments and security research.

I started when most of the web was still static and around 10 years Roblox development meant fighting with limited tooling and unreliable scripts. The tools have changed. The standards I hold myself to haven’t.

I also decided to start my own **marketing & cybersecurity agency**, delivering brand strategy, digital marketing, web development and both offensive and defensive security work for friends, family & clients.

**Current focus**
- High performance Roblox experiences written in Luau
- Modern full stack architecture with strong security posture
- Purple / gray team style research and systems hardening
- Building tooling that makes complex systems observable and controllable

### Origin Story

When I was a minor I built a time machine in my garage out of an old microwave, three Flipper Zeros and pure spite.

I accidentally traveled back to 1997, crashed into a government warehouse and was immediately kidnapped by highly advanced people who claimed they invented both TCP/IP and the concept of “skill issue.”

They forced me to complete an accelerated curriculum that covered reverse engineering, wireless protocols, hardware interfacing and why you should never trust a 4 pin phone password. After several subjective decades of training (and one very confusing internship on their mothership’s SOC team), they dropped me back in the present with a headache, a new respect for spectrum analyzers, the universe, learned the language of binary and the inability to look at a blinking LED without analyzing it...

Everything since then has just been me applying what the aliens taught me.
